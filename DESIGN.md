## Brand & Style

This design system channels an adrenaline-fueled, high-stakes competitive esports aura tailored specifically for a premier Fortnite Battle Royale tournament in Colombia. It fuses brutalist digital precision with hyper-refined glassmorphism, delivering an experience that feels electric, authoritative, and battle-ready.

The visual style is characterized by deep, nocturnal layered obsidian surfaces punctuated by striking bursts of cybernetic cyan and energized ultraviolet illumination. Visual metaphors derive from tactical storm trackers, dynamic zone shields, and tournament HUD overlays:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian tones evoke competitive intensity, while razor-sharp neon gradients inject kinetic energy and forward momentum.
- **Target Audience:** Competitive and semi-professional gamers, regional esports clans, content creators, and youth gaming culture spanning mobile, console, and PC communities across Latin America.
- **Emotional Response:** Intense competition, prestige, speed, pride, and the hunger for tactical victory and prize pool dominance.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for deep-contrast low-light environments, prioritizing readability, tactical hierarchy, and focused vibrancy.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#8B5CF6` / `#A855F7`):** Electric Purple. The primary energy vector used for key interactive states, active tournament triggers, and primary brand markers.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` / `#38BDF8`):** Cyber Cyan. Represents real-time data, telemetry, live indicators, squad status, and secondary CTA accents.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24`):** Victory Gold. Reserved strictly for 1st-place trophies, prize pool highlights, tier-1 rewards, and champion statuses.
- **Neutral Canvas:**
  - Base Obsidian: `#0B0E14` (root viewport canvas)
  - Surface Mid: `#121824` (structural containers and structural panels)
  - Surface Elevate: `#1A2234` (glass cards, popovers, and interactive modules)
  - Surface Highlight: `#243048` (card borders, dividers, subtle hover fills)

### Utility & Functional Colors
- **Success / Zone Active:** `#10B981` (registration open, player confirmed, online ping)
- **Destructive / Storm Danger:** `#EF4444` (eliminated, squad full, registration closed)
- **Text & Foreground:**
  - High Emphasis: `#F8FAFC`
  - Medium Emphasis: `#94A3B8`
  - Disabled / Low Emphasis: `#475569`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear battlefield telemetry through an intentional tri-font strategy:
- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Highly stylized, sharp, and geometric. Delivers visceral impact in hero titles, tournament countdown headers, and bracket stages. Uppercase treatment is strongly encouraged for display titles and stage headings.
- **Body & Editorial (`Chivo`):** High-velocity, ultra-crisp sans-serif built for rapid scan speed across tournament rules, eligibility guidelines, and schedule blocks.
- **Telemetry & Labels (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to technical data points—prize pool amounts (COP / USD), live kill counters, slot limits, timestamps, server tick rates, and registration IDs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column responsive grid engineered for dense tournament metadata and dynamic real-time streams.

### Grid Anatomy & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (`< 768px`):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (16px), `gutter-sm` (16px). All tournament brackets, stats, and rule tiers stack vertically into single-column modules.
- **Tablet (`768px – 1024px`):** 8 columns, `margin` (32px), `gutter-sm` (16px). Dual-column leaderboard cards and side-by-side registration forms.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12 columns, max-width 1360px centered, `margin` (32px), `gutter` (24px). Accommodates multi-column brackets, live stream embedded players, and synchronized countdown columns.

### Spacing Principles
- Internal card padding strictly utilizes `space-md` on mobile and `space-lg` on desktop.
- Inter-section spacing commands breathing room (`space-xl` scaled up to `4rem` on desktop displays) to prevent neon fatigue and allow glass panels to retain visual separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established not through opaque elevation levels, but through a dynamic interplay of dark translucent glass layers, backdrop blurs, and colored illumination bleed.

### Glassmorphic Layers
- **Ground Floor:** Deep `#0B0E14` base background, with subtle diagonal mesh gradients in deep purple and cyan (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08)` and `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.05)`).
- **Floating Containers:** `rgba(18, 24, 36, 0.75)` backdrop blur (`16px`), outlined with a continuous crisp 1px stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Active / Focused Layer:** `rgba(26, 34, 52, 0.90)` backdrop blur (`24px`), complemented by an exterior 1px neon fringe border `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`.

### Neon Glow & Lighting
- **Cyan Glow:** Applied to live interactive elements and dynamic pills: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -4px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`.
- **Purple Radiance:** Applied to primary CTAs and hero badge rings: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -2px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.45)`.
- **Champion Gold Halo:** Applied exclusively to Tier-1 prize nodes: `box-shadow: 0 0 32px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design balances esports tactical geometry with smooth, contemporary ergonomics. 
- Elements employ a base radius of `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons, inputs, and badge tags.
- Structural cards and modal viewports use `rounded-lg` (16px) or `rounded-xl` (24px) for expansive framing.
- Interactive pills and status badges utilize complete radius wrapping (`9999px`) to starkly contrast against the structured grid boundaries.
- Angled cuts (45-degree chamfers) can be selectively deployed on secondary button corners and leaderboard ranking flags via CSS clip-path to infuse competitive Fortnite tactical aesthetics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Register / Drop In):** Electric purple gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #8B5CF6 0%, #7C3AED 100%)`), uppercase `Space Grotesk` bold typography, high-contrast white text, `0.5rem` radius, and a constant purple ambient rim-glow that expands on hover.
- **Secondary Action (Watch Stream / Rules):** Obsidian glass base (`rgba(26, 34, 52, 0.8)`), 1px cyan outline (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.5)`), cyan text, hovering smoothly into full cyan wash with dark ink foreground.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Transparent base, slate text (`#94A3B8`), subtle underline hover effect.

### Glass Cards (Brackets, Matchups, Rules)
- Structured panels backed by `rgba(18, 24, 36, 0.7)` with `16px` backdrop blur.
- Outer border: 1px gradient stroke descending from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` at top-left to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` at bottom-right.
- Hover state: Border stroke highlights into vibrant cyan or violet with an elevation lift of `-2px`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Live Match Indicator:** Pill-shaped with a pulsating dot (`#10B981`), monospace font, emerald glow.
- **Server / Region Tag:** `JetBrains Mono`, all-caps, `0.25rem` padding, subtle slate background with electric cyan text (`COLOMBIA // LATAM NORTH`).
- **Prize Tiers:** Gold-accented badge for 1st place with victory icon and `JetBrains Mono` prize values.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs:** Dark obsidian wells (`#0B0E14`), inset border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Focus transitions to a sharp cyan stroke (`#06B6D4`) with a localized cyan ambient glow. Placeholder in muted slate (`#475569`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp geometric squares or circles with deep slate borders; selected state fills with electric purple and displays an obsidian battle checkmark.

### Esports-Specific Components
- **Countdown HUD Timer:** Segmented digital boxes with monospace digits (`JetBrains Mono`), slate label dividers (DÍAS, HORAS, MIN, SEG), and glowing digit tops.
- **Prize Pool Podium Cards:** Three-tier comparative podium highlighting 1st Place in center with Victory Gold accents, 2nd Place in Cyber Cyan, and 3rd Place in Electric Purple.
- **Squad Roster Slot:** Horizontal list element showing Epic Games ID, country flag token, seed rank, and ping indicator.
